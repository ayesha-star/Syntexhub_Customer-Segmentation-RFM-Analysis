# Key Business Insights

Loyal customers contributed the highest share of total revenue despite representing a smaller portion of the customer base.
Regular customers formed the largest customer group and generated steady recurring revenue.
New customers showed recent activity but lower spending, indicating an opportunity for onboarding and cross-selling campaigns.
Churn-risk customers had not purchased for a long period and should be targeted with retention offers.
Electronics and Clothing were the strongest product categories by revenue.
Bengaluru and Ahmedabad were the top-performing cities in terms of sales.

# Marketing Recommendations

Loyal Customers :
   Offer VIP rewards
   Provide early access to sales
   Give exclusive discounts

Regular Customers :
   Encourage repeat purchases
   Recommend related products
   Use loyalty points

New Customers :
   Send welcome emails
   Offer first-purchase coupons
   Provide product recommendations

Churn Risk :
   Launch win-back campaigns
   Offer personalized discounts
   Ask for feedback
